About

An engineering-led defence initiative.

Anmay Defence is an initiative by Robonut Project. Every system we show has been designed, built, measured and re-built by the same small team — mechanical, electronics, software and the people who operate it.

Pune, IndiaIndigenous designPrototype to product

Approach

Engineering comes first.

We started with a problem our own environment makes obvious: small drones are cheap, quiet and hard to see, and the systems meant to stop them are often slow, fragmented or imported. So we built the whole stack ourselves — the mount, the actuators, the drives, the electronics and the software that ties them together.

  • Prototypes are measured, documented and re-machined rather than outsourced
  • Mechanical, electronics and computer vision developed as one system
  • Operator clarity treated as a design requirement, not a UI afterthought
  • Claims limited to what has actually been built and tested

Milestones

How the build progressed

Taken from the dates carried by the archive itself.

  1. July 2025

    Trigger actuation

    Servo brackets fitted to AK receivers, measured with a steel rule; the five-unit trigger concept takes shape.

  2. Late July – August 2025

    Drives and structure

    Stepper driver, slew column with spur gear and bearing plate, elevation hardware, machined pulleys.

  3. October 2025

    On the vehicle

    Mast, mount and console installed on the 4×4; command station wired on the flatbed.

  4. 2026

    Trial grounds

    Field photography and drone footage of the complete system; STRIKE M multi-weapon build on the bench.

  5. Next

    Gen2 and qualification

    Layered defence with day/night PTZ, LiDAR and edge AI; compliance targets for defence procurement.
