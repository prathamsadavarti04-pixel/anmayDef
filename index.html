The threat

Drone threats are evolving. Airspace protection has to keep up.

Small, fast, low-altitude UAVs are cheap, commercially available and increasingly autonomous. They disrupt secure facilities, critical infrastructure, public events and industrial operations — and a single countermeasure may not cover every scenario.

01

Low-altitude threats

Small drones are hard to see, tell apart and follow across large sites, especially below conventional radar coverage.

02

Fragmented awareness

Separate sensors produce separate pictures. Operators need one view, not four.

03

Delayed decisions

When alerts arrive late or unclear, the window for a proportionate response closes.

The approach

One integrated counter-drone workflow

STRIKE brings detection, tracking, threat prioritisation and response support into a single operational view — on a vehicle, on a mast, or on a fixed installation.

How the system works

Platform family

One platform. Multiple configurations.

The same base platform carries different sensors, effectors and mission packages, so capability grows without replacing the hardware.

STRIKE Gen1

AI-assisted detection and tracking with a five-point modular mounting platform. Built for rapid deployment on vehicle or static mounts.

STRIKE Gen2 concept

Layered defence: kinetic and electronic, with day/night PTZ optics, LiDAR, edge AI and extended communications.

STRIKE M

Multi-weapon fit-out: the same mount carrying mixed effectors, demonstrated with two rifles and two belt-fed machine guns.

In the field

Designed for real deployment, not showroom conditions

Vehicle-mounted, static and mast-based configurations have been built and exercised on site — including a tracked-rover concept for autonomous deployment.

  • Rapid mast deployment from the vehicle bed
  • Operator console with manual and automatic engagement modes
  • Modular mission packages: RF early warning, optical fibre, electronic warfare, solar power, multi-weapon
  • Day and night operation, with thermal-ready architecture
