STRIKE-M · field configuration

The problem

Small drones changed the shape of the threat.

Low altitude, small radar cross-section, autonomy and swarm tactics make conventional surveillance incomplete. Detection alone does not create a decision — and a decision alone does not create a response.

Hard to see

Small, fast and low-flying targets slip under traditional coverage.

Hard to trust

Separate sensors produce separate pictures, not one operational view.

Hard to act on

Late or unclear alerts cost the operator the seconds that matter.

Hard to adapt

Fixed architectures cannot absorb new sensors, payloads or missions.

STRIKE Gen 1 turret · five independent trigger units

The platform family

One platform. Multiple generations. Mission-specific configurations.

STRIKE is modular by design: a common turret and control architecture that takes on new sensors, effectors and deployment forms as the mission changes.

How it works

From a pixel to a decision.

The platform closes the loop in four linked stages, keeping a human operator in control of the response at every step.

01

Detect

Computer vision identifies aerial activity inside protected airspace.

02

Track

Continuous position and heading of every aerial object in view.

03

Prioritise

Proximity and movement patterns separate routine traffic from threats.

04

Respond

Alerts, cueing and effector control for a rapid, deliberate response.
